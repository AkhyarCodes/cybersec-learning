# Incident Response (IR), Business Continuity (BC), and Disaster Recovery (DR) Concepts

## Incident Terminologies

| Terminology | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| Personally Identifiable Information (PII) | Info that can be used to trace individual's identity | 
| Breach | unauthorized disclosure of data |
| Event | anything observable that happen in networks |
| Incident | events that jeopardizes the CIA triad of systems |
| Intrusion | unauthorized user gaining access (the proecss itself) |
| Zero Day | unknown vulnerablities, leaving the developers zero day to fix it before the vulnerablity being exploited |

The goal of **Incident Response** is to protect safety, health, and life.

**Business Continuity**:  
most companies store their data digitally (almost no hardcopy)
they give *red book* to someone outside the facility  
the book stores the digital information physically (hardcopy)  
the book will undergo frequent updates to align with the digital info
the book also contain prodcedures in case electronic access in unavailable  

**Business Continuity Planning (BCP)** is a procesure that is followed by all members in an organization.
